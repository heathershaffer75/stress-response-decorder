# 🧠 Stress Response Decoder

A trauma-informed **Primary Mental Health (PMH)** group activity that helps patients understand:

> **“Why do I react like this under stress?”**

This tool explains how the nervous system responds to perceived threat and teaches patients how to recognize, understand, and respond to their patterns more effectively.

---

## 🧠 Purpose

This app supports:

* Awareness of stress responses
* Understanding automatic reactions
* Reducing shame around behavior
* Emotional and physiological regulation
* Building safer, more intentional responses

---

## 🎯 Core Concept

When the brain detects stress or threat, it shifts into survival mode.

These responses are:

* automatic
* protective
* learned over time

> **They are not character flaws—they are survival strategies.**

---

## 🔥 Stress Responses Included

* 🔥 **Fight** – push back, control, react
* 🏃 **Flight** – escape, avoid, stay busy
* 🧊 **Freeze** – shut down, stuck, disconnected
* 🤝 **Fawn** – people-please, avoid conflict
* 🌑 **Shutdown** – withdraw, low energy, collapse

---

## 🔍 Each Response Includes

* **Simple explanation**
* **What it looks like (behavior)**
* **What it feels like internally**
* **What helps regulate it**
* **What the response may need**
* **Discussion questions for group use**

---

## 🧑‍⚕️ How to Use in Group

### Option 1: Self-Identification

* Ask: “Which response do you notice most in yourself?”
* Explore that response in detail

---

### Option 2: Psychoeducation

* Walk through each response
* Help patients recognize patterns
* Normalize reactions

---

### Option 3: Pattern Awareness

* Ask:

  * “When does this show up?”
  * “What triggers it?”
  * “What helps shift it?”

---

## 💡 Why This Works

* Normalizes behavior instead of pathologizing it
* Helps patients understand *why* they react
* Creates space between trigger and response
* Encourages curiosity instead of judgment
* Provides clear, actionable tools
